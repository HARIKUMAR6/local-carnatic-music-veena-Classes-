# Image attribution and licence record (internal)

Internal project documentation. There is **no public photo-credits section, footer credit or hidden credits UI**. Keep this file for licence and rights tracking.

## Status after the Phase 3 licence-compliance pass (2026-11-06)

All 20 image slots on Home 1 and Home 2 were re-sourced so that **every photograph is CC0 or public domain, with no attribution requirement**. The earlier CC BY / CC BY-SA photographs were removed from the site (19 files). The replacements come from 18 distinct source photographs: two source photographs (the Kerala concert photo and the vocalist-with-tanpura photo) are each used twice, once per homepage (different crops), because the CC0/public-domain pool of genuine Carnatic photographs is small.

Audit method: each file's Wikimedia Commons description page, metadata, wikitext and categories were read via the public MediaWiki API. "Verified" means **Commons states this licence and source**. I did not contact the authors. None of these files carries a Commons restriction tag (the `Restrictions` field is empty for all 18). Absence of a tag is not proof that rights are cleared.

Rights notes
- Licence and "own work" are the uploader's declarations on Commons; the originals were not independently verified, and no personality-rights restriction tag was present on any file.
- Seven photographs show real, identifiable musicians (about-hero-duo, about-hero-mridangam, about-hero-lecdem, courses-hero-vocal, courses-vocal-practice, teachers-hero-arangetram, about-learning-students). They are **illustrative only**. They are not captioned with names, and none may be presented as academy instructors or students. CC0 does not remove personality or publicity rights; replace them with the academy's own photographs before launch.
- courses-hero-veena is a museum object photograph (The Met Open Access, CC0): an instrument, not a performance.
- contact-mylapore and the two Nagaraja temple photographs show real places and are illustrative. The Nagaraja temple is in Nagercoil, Tamil Nadu, **not** in Chennai and not the academy. The Mylapore photograph is captioned as illustrative.
- Candidate rejected: "Aishwarya Raghunath.JPG" (CC0 on Commons but transferred from English Wikipedia without a stated original source). Not used.
- **No suitable CC0 photograph of a Carnatic veena being played was found.** The veena visuals are an instrument object photograph and the Home 1 close-up. If a performance photograph is wanted, it must be supplied or commissioned.
- The instructor portraits remain instrument photographs (see "Subjects" above): the provisional instructors have no real photographs.

## Rights that copyright licences do NOT cover (unresolved)

A CC0 or public-domain licence waives *copyright*. It does not grant **personality, publicity or privacy rights** of the people pictured. Commons documents no model release or consent for any of these photographs.

- **Clear of people:** Met museum objects (neutral-1, neutral-2, neutral-3, course-veena-closeup, h2-philosophy-tambura, h2-course-veena).
- **Historic, low risk but not formally cleared:** hero-veena-performance (1921 plate), academy-traditional-setting (1948 group), h2-benefits-portrait (older portrait).
- **Identifiable living people, rights NOT cleared:** course-carnatic-vocal (and h2-cta-vocalist), learning-lecture-demonstration, why-mridangam-artist, stage-carnatic-concert, student-concert-stage (and h2-course-vocal), h2-hero-vocalist-tanpura, h2-journey-practice, h2-stage-concert, h2-voices-ensemble. These are public performers or event photographs, but using them to promote an academy may imply endorsement. **Before launch, replace them with photographs of your own teachers and students (with written consent), or obtain consent from the people shown.**
- No photograph is presented as depicting an academy instructor or student. Instructor frames use neutral museum-object photographs.
- Excluded on purpose: a photo of a child performer (arangetram), a photo tagged with a Commons personality-rights restriction, paintings, and any file whose licence required attribution.
